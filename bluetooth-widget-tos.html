The app

Bluetooth Widget displays your paired and nearby Bluetooth devices, including their connection status, signal strength, and battery level when reported by the device, on an Android home screen widget. Tapping a device opens Android's own Bluetooth connection panel to connect, and saved devices can be unpaired from the widget after a confirmation step. The widget refreshes automatically when Bluetooth states change and can be resized. The app is provided for personal, noncommercial use, and works on Android 12 and above.
